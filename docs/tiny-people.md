# The Tiny People: Ghibli Scavengers

**Concept:** 
The Tiny People are pixelated, Studio Ghibli-style micro-humanoids fighting to survive against "The Void." Moving away from voxel-based geometry, they are rendered as **expressive 2D sprites** that billboard within the 3D world. Their survival relies on micro-engineering and scavenging, blending whimsical aesthetics with gritty industrial survival.

## Visual Aesthetic: Pixelated Ghibli
The goal is to capture the charm of 90s-era pixel art combined with the soft, pastoral, yet technically detailed look of Studio Ghibli films (e.g., *Castle in the Sky*, *The Secret World of Arrietty*).

- **Proportions**: Slightly oversized heads and eyes for readability.
- **Animations**: Fluid, frame-by-frame pixel animations for walking, climbing, and emoting.
- **Transparency**: Sprites use alpha-testing to maintain crisp pixel edges without "boxiness."
- **Lighting**: Sprites receive world lighting and cast subtle "blob" or pixelated shadows to ground them in the 3D space.

## Regional & Climate Variations

### 1. The Urban Concrete Scavengers (NYC Temperate Biome)
* **Home:** Dense urban grids, sidewalks, pre-war apartments.
* **Materials:** Asphalt crumbs, shattered glass dust, rust flakes, concrete powder, and pigeon down.
* **Apparel:** Gritty, functional armor woven from lint and pet hair. They use tiny shards of glass as visors or cutting tools. 
* **Climate Adaptation (Winter):** During the winter (when "thermodynamic drain" makes the Void highly aggressive), they bundle up in heavy lint-weaves and scavenged wool fibers to insulate their fragile tech.
* **Vibe:** Industrial, vertical climbers, tough survivalists.

### 2. The Suburban / Forest Foragers (e.g., Pacific Northwest)
* **Home:** Parks, backyards, wooded areas (`LC_GRASSLAND` & `LC_TREE_COVER`).
* **Materials:** Pine needles, beetle husks, dried moss, and coarse dirt.
* **Apparel:** Cloaks woven from spider silk and leaves. Their gear is highly organic, camouflaging them perfectly against the natural terrain.
* **Climate Adaptation (Heavy Rain):** They engineer waterproof ponchos out of micro-plastics (candy wrappers, bits of bags) washed in by the rain, using surface tension to their advantage.
* **Vibe:** Ranger-like, stealthy, organic engineers.

### 3. The Arid / Sun-Bleached Nomads (e.g., Southwest US)
* **Home:** Dry, hot climates, sand, baked pavement.
* **Materials:** Sand grains, dried clay, sun-baked debris.
* **Apparel:** Lightweight, breathable wraps. They utilize reflective scraps (bits of aluminum foil or metallic paint flakes) to deflect intense heat and sunlight.
* **Climate Adaptation (Extreme Heat):** Their tech relies on capturing solar energy. They build "sand-skimmers" to cross hot pavement quickly before the heat damages their fragile systems.
* **Vibe:** Desert scrappers, fast, solar-powered.

### 4. The Coastal / Deep Water Dwellers (e.g., Miami, Lakefronts)
* **Home:** Beaches, docks, riverbanks (`LC_WATER` margins).
* **Materials:** Smooth micro-pebbles, dried algae, bits of eroded sea-glass, fish scales.
* **Apparel:** Sleek, hydrodynamic gear. They utilize the hollow structures of tiny shells as natural armor against the Void.
* **Climate Adaptation (High Humidity):** Their tech is sealed with scavenged sap or tar to prevent water damage. They are the most adept at navigating near water blocks.
* **Vibe:** Amphibious, sleek, heavily armored.

## The Pocket Dimension Dynamics
This regional identity makes the **Pocket Dimension** transport mechanic visually striking. When a User transports their Urban Scavengers to a Coastal biome, the gritty, glass-wielding New Yorkers will run alongside local shell-armored Coastal Dwellers. You can instantly identify where a Tiny Person originated based on their scavenged gear.

## Tech Progression: Void Suits
As a colony advances and constructs larger defenses (like Lidar Towers and Void Thrusters), their gear homogenizes slightly. They upgrade from raw scavenged debris into sleek, glowing, geometric "Void Suits." However, a high-level suit will always retain an accent of their home zip code (e.g., a pristine Void Suit that incorporates a glowing piece of sea-glass or a reinforced beetle husk).

## Facial Features & Expressions
When their scavenged visors or helmets are off, the Tiny People have slightly animated, cartoonish facial expressions. Their proportions feature slightly larger eyes and expressive faces to ensure readability at a micro-scale. This allows them to effectively emote and communicate non-verbally when the User interacts with them via voice commands, adding emotional weight and personality to their survival journey.
