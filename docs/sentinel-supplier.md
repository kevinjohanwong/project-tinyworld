# The Supplier: The Great Sentinel

**The Supplier** is a colossal entity that exists as the heart and protector of every Tiny World. Standing at approximately 10 to 15 blocks tall (the size of a multi-story building relative to the Tiny People), it represents the User's presence and influence within the simulation.

## Behavior & States

### 1. The Active State (User Controlling)
When the User is actively controlling the world (connected via the app), the Supplier "wakes up." 
- **Animation**: It slowly unfolds from its resting position, shifting from a landscape feature into a towering mechanical or stone-like guardian.
- **Role**: In this state, it acts as a beacon of safety. Its presence suppresses "The Void" in a large radius and provides the interface for the User's high-level commands (Building, Scanning, Pressing).
- **Interaction**: The Tiny People flock toward it when it wakes, viewing it as a provider of material and security.

### 2. The Dormant State (User Absent)
When the User leaves the simulation, the Supplier "sits back down."
- **Visuals**: It tucks its limbs and head, settling into a form that resembles a hill, a rock formation, or an ancient, overgrown ruin.
- **Integration**: It becomes a passive part of the landscape. Moss and small plants might grow on its surface over time.
- **Void Vulnerability**: Without the Supplier active, the world's protection levels drop, and the Tiny People must rely on the structures they built while the Supplier was awake to survive the night.

## Gameplay Significance
- **Resource Hub**: The Supplier is the primary point where "Mass" (Void matter) is processed into useful materials.
- **Strategic Anchor**: Users often build their most critical structures near the Supplier's resting place to take advantage of its passive protection and the "Home" vibe it provides.
- **Lore**: Ancient stories among the Tiny People suggest the Supplier is a "Sentinel from the Cloud," a noble steed that carries their world through the Void.

## Visual Design
- **Scale**: 10-15 voxels tall.
- **Aesthetic**: A blend of ancient stone tectonics and futuristic, glowing "Void" circuitry. In its sitting position, it should be almost indistinguishable from the natural terrain at a distance.
