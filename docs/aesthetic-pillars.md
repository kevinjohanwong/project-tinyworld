# Aesthetic & Animation Pillars

**Core Inspirations:** *Hades, NieR: Automata, Transistor, The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess*

Project Quantum blends the gritty, physical reality of LiDAR scans with highly stylized, expressive characters and dark, moody atmospheric effects.

## 1. The "Supergiant" Pop & Snap (Hades / Transistor)
* **Visual Contrast:** The physical LiDAR world is often muted and realistic. The Tiny People and their technology provide a striking contrast using vibrant, glowing neon accents (like Red's sword in *Transistor* or the underworld colors in *Hades*).
* **Animation Style:** At a micro-scale, realistic animation gets lost. Movement should be snappy, fluid, and rely on exaggerated key poses. 
* **Dash & Trails:** When Tiny People move quickly or fight the Void, they leave brief, glowing "smear" trails of light behind them, making their actions highly readable even when zoomed out.

## 2. The Melancholic Machine (NieR: Automata)
* **Tech vs. Ruin:** The Tiny People wear scavenged, rusted, and organic debris, but their core tech (and eventual Void Suits) has the sleek, elegant, almost heartbreaking beauty of YoRHa androids contrasting with rusted machine lifeforms.
* **The "Stranded" Vibe:** When left behind in the physical world, their idle animations reflect a beautiful melancholy. They might sit down, wrap their lint-woven cloaks around their knees, and stare up at the massive, empty human world. 

## 3. The Twilight Void (Twilight Princess)
* **The Threat:** "The Void" isn't just empty space or generic darkness. Drawing heavily from the Twilight Realm, the Void is an eerie, creeping corruption. It features floating, glowing geometric particles (squares and shattered polygons) that slowly eat away at the scanned world.
* **The Atmosphere:** When the Void encroaches (especially at night), the lighting shifts. The colors desaturate, shadows deepen, and the only light comes from the Tiny People's glowing tech and the eerie bioluminescence of Void creatures.

## 4. Expressive Micro-Pantomime (Non-Verbal Communication)
Because they are tiny, their non-verbal communication must be theatrical and distinct:
* **Hunger / Low Satiation:** Their glowing accents dim. They drag their scavenged weapons heavily, their posture slumps (like 2B at low health), and they look up at the camera/User with large, expressive eyes.
* **Danger / Void Approaching:** Instead of generic panic, they snap into a sharp, combat-ready stance (like Zagreus). Their weapons ignite, and they point sharply toward the threat, signaling the User.
* **Joy / Task Complete:** A stylized, triumphant weapon flourish or an exaggerated jump that leaves a little burst of glowing particles.
