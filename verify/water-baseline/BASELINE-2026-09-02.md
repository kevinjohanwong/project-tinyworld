## Baseline observations relevant to the persistence change
1. Every load cold-starts water from the spring + 90s warm-up; nothing about the
   previous session's pool survives a reload (warmupLeft resets, count 0).
2. Water state that exists to persist: per-column spans from `waterCells()`
   (fine voxel coords), the ledger counters in `report.report`, spring meta.
3. Hidden/background tabs freeze the sim entirely (no catch-up).
